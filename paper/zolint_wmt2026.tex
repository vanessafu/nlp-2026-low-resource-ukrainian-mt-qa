\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{Zolint}, our submission to the Ukrainian track of the
WMT~2026 Shared Task on Multitask LLMs with Limited Resources.
Our system is a two-stage LoRA adaptation of
\texttt{Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B}, covering machine translation (English/Czech$\to$Ukrainian),
question answering, spelling and grammar correction, and math reasoning.
The released adapter is available at
\href{https://huggingface.co/MDK-YLC/Qwen3.5-2B-WMT26-Ukrainian-LoRA}{\texttt{MDK-YLC/Qwen3.5-2B-WMT26-Ukrainian-LoRA}}.
On the official test set, our unified system obtains the best score on all
reported metrics except question answering.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method (summary)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stage~1:} LoRA ($r{=}16$, $\alpha{=}32$) on MT (en$\to$uk) + GC + SC for up to 5~epochs;
  select the best checkpoint by combined dev MT+GC+SC (typically epoch~1).
  \item \textbf{Stage~2:} Continue LoRA for 1~epoch on QA + MR with anti-forgetting
  replay (MT/GC samples) and full Czech$\to$Ukrainian parallel chat data.
  \item \textbf{Inference:} Task-specific chat prompts; export predictions in the
  official \texttt{Ukrainian/} JSONL layout.
\end{enumerate}
